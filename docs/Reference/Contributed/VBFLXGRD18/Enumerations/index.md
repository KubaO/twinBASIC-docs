# VBFLXGRD18 Enumerations
{: .no_toc }

The 61 public enumerations of the package are described on three pages, grouped by the topic pages of the [**VBFlexGrid**](../VBFlexGrid/) control that use them. All of them are declared inside the control, and their members can be written with or without the name of the enumeration, as in `FlexSortStringAscending` or `FlexSortConstants.FlexSortStringAscending`.

- [Cells, Text and Appearance](CellsAndAppearance) -- 25 enumerations: Rows and Columns, Sizes and Positions, Cell Text, Cell Formatting, Merging Cells, Appearance.
- [Editing, Combo Boxes and Check Boxes](EditingAndComboBoxes) -- 13 enumerations: Editing Cells, Drop-Down Lists, Buttons and Calendars, Check Boxes, Data Binding.
- [Selection, Mouse, Sorting and Clipboard](SelectionAndInput) -- 23 enumerations: Current Cell and Selection, Scrolling, Mouse, Keyboard and Drag and Drop, Sorting and Searching, Clipboard, Undo and Arrays.

[FlexDataSourceFlags](EditingAndComboBoxes#flexdatasourceflags) is a set of flags that combine with **Or**. The values of [FlexAllowUserFreezingConstants](CellsAndAppearance#flexallowuserfreezingconstants), [FlexAllowUserResizingConstants](CellsAndAppearance#flexallowuserresizingconstants) and [FlexClipCopyModeConstants](SelectionAndInput#flexclipcopymodeconstants) are also made of bits, and their sections say which.
